Hi! I'm currently an undergraduate at Harvard studying Applied Math in Computer Science and Economics.
I'm broadly interested in how AI can support high-impact decisions in settings where the world is uncertain, the data are imperfect, and the objectives may be contested.
I've approached this from several angles, from computational biology to subseasonal weather forecasting, and currently, symbolic continual learning for generalizable agents.

Please reach out to chat about research or anything else! Email me: hguan [at] college [dot] harvard [dot] edu
