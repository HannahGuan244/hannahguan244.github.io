Advancing Subseasonal Forecasting with Machine Learning

Hannah Guan*, Soukayna Mouatadid*, Paulo Orenstein, Judah Cohen, Haiyu Dong, Zekun Ni, Jeremy Berman, Genevieve Flaspohler, Alex Lu, Jakob Schloer, Joshua Talib, Jonathan A. Weyn, Lester Mackey

ICLR 2026 Workshop on Machine Learning for Remote Sensing

Built Probabilistic Bias Correction (PBC), a machine learning framework that corrects systematic bias in subseasonal weather forecasts (2-6 week lead times) by learning to adjust historical probabilistic forecasts. Applied to ECMWF's leading dynamical and AI models, PBC doubles the AI Forecasting System's subseasonal skill and improves the debiased dynamical model's skill for the vast majority of pressure, temperature, and precipitation targets. In ECMWF's 2025 real-time forecasting competition, it placed first across all variables and lead times, ahead of six national forecasting centers and 34 competing teams worldwide. Better subseasonal forecasts have real implications for agriculture, water management, and disaster preparedness.
