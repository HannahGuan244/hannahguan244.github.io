Research

Advancing Subseasonal Forecasting with Machine Learning

ICLR 2026 Workshop on Machine Learning for Remote Sensing

Built Probabilistic Bias Correction (PBC), a machine learning framework that corrects systematic bias in subseasonal weather forecasts (2–6 week lead times) by learning to adjust historical probabilistic forecasts rather than raw point predictions. Applied to ECMWF's operational models, it roughly doubled forecasting skill for temperature and pressure and substantially improved precipitation forecasts, with real implications for agriculture, water management, and disaster preparedness.
